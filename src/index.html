Customer groups

The processor saves each card on file under its own customer name, so one customer can appear several times (for example “Fig Deli”, “Fig Deli OTO” and “Fig Deli 4242”). Group those names and the whole dashboard treats them as one customer. Manual charges with no customer name can be added too, by their cardholder. Groups are kept in this browser and applied to every export you open.

Customer notes

The export only shows card payments. It can’t tell a customer who stopped ordering from one who pays by EFT now, or a monthly payer from a weekly one who is late. Record what you know here and every list applies it: Pays by EFT or cheque marks them as stopped paying by card (with the reason); Cycle monthly compares them month to month, seasonal never flags them, sporadic or on request means no rhythm to be late against; Status stopped ordering or closed moves them to Lost customers. Notes are kept in this browser; save the file to keep a copy or edit it in a spreadsheet.
